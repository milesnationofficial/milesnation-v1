From finish to doorstep

Your effort, recognised.

Clear submission guidelines and delivery details help you know what happens after your finish.

How we verify

Submit a screenshot or activity record from a running, walking, or cycling app, or a GPS-enabled fitness tracker, showing your completed activity details. Follow the challenge-specific submission instructions.

Our team reviews submissions within the stated verification period. If any details need clarification, we will contact you using your registration details.

How your medal arrives

Your finisher medal is included in the limited-slot offer. Provide a complete delivery address and contact details during registration.

After your activity is approved, your medal will be shipped to your registered address. Delivery time can vary by destination.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before choosing your challenge.

Is this a physical event?
No. MilesNation challenges are virtual. You complete your activity on your own route and schedule, without travelling to an event venue.
When can I complete it?
Complete your selected activity within the challenge window shown after registration. You can choose when and where to exercise during that period, following the challenge rules.
What proof do I submit?
Submit an activity screenshot or record from a running, walking, or cycling app that clearly shows your completed activity and relevant details. Your submission must meet the challenge verification requirements.
Is the medal included in the price?
Yes. The limited-slot launch offer is ₹299 and includes both your digital certificate and finisher medal.
What is the refund policy?
Please review the refund terms shown during registration before completing your payment.
How do I contact you?
For questions about registration, submissions, or delivery, contact the MilesNation team using the contact details provided during registration.
