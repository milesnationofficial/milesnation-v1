01 / WHO WE ARE

About Us

MilesNation is a virtual fitness challenge platform created to encourage people to stay active, healthy and motivated through physical activities.

We believe that fitness is not limited by location, age or competition. Everyone should have an opportunity to challenge themselves, achieve personal goals and celebrate their progress.

What We Offer

  • Virtual running challenges.
  • Virtual walking challenges.
  • Virtual cycling challenges.
  • Finisher medals for eligible participants.
  • Digital certificates of achievement.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make fitness accessible, enjoyable and motivating by bringing people together through virtual fitness challenges.

Every mile matters. Every effort counts. Your fitness journey deserves recognition.

03 / PARTICIPATION RULES

Terms & Conditions

By registering for a MilesNation challenge, participants agree to the following terms and conditions.

1. Registration

  • Participants must provide accurate registration information.
  • Participants must select the correct activity and distance category.
  • Registration is considered successful after payment confirmation.
  • Registration details should be reviewed before submission.

2. Participation

  • Participants are responsible for completing their selected challenge.
  • Participants should follow safe practices during physical activities.
  • Participants must not submit false or misleading activity records.
  • Participants should follow the event-specific instructions published by MilesNation.

3. Health and Safety

Participants are responsible for assessing their physical fitness before taking part. Consult a qualified medical professional if you have concerns about your ability to participate.

MilesNation is not a substitute for medical advice, supervision or emergency services.

4. Medals and Certificates

Finisher medals and digital certificates are subject to the applicable event rules and successful completion requirements.

5. Event Changes

MilesNation reserves the right to modify event schedules, instructions or arrangements when reasonably necessary. Participants will be informed of significant changes through available communication channels.

6. Acceptance

Completing registration indicates that the participant has read and agreed to these terms and the applicable event instructions.

04 / PAYMENT POLICY

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Important: MilesNation registration fees are non-refundable once registration is successfully completed, except for the payment-related exceptions described below.

1. Non-Refundable Registrations

Once a participant successfully registers for an event, the registration fee is non-refundable.

Personal reasons, changes in plans, inability to participate or failure to complete the challenge do not qualify for a refund.

2. Duplicate Payments

If a participant is charged more than once for the same registration, the duplicate payment can be reported to MilesNation for verification and resolution.

3. Failed Transactions

If a payment fails but money is deducted from the participant's account, the participant should contact MilesNation with the transaction reference and payment proof. Verified cases will be handled through the payment provider's applicable reversal or refund process.

4. Event Cancellation by MilesNation

If MilesNation cancels an event, affected participants will be informed about the available resolution. Where a refund is applicable, it will be processed through the original payment method, subject to verification.

5. How to Request Payment Assistance

Email milesnationofficial@gmail.com with your registered email address, payment reference and relevant screenshots.

Verified payment issues will be reviewed and handled according to the applicable payment provider process.

05 / YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

MilesNation respects the privacy of its participants. This policy explains the information we collect and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

  • First name and last name.
  • Email address and contact number, where collected.
  • Postal and delivery address.
  • Selected activity and challenge category.
  • Registration and payment transaction references.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process registrations and confirm participation.
  • To communicate event updates and important information.
  • To arrange medal and certificate delivery.
  • To respond to customer support requests.
  • To maintain registration and transaction records.

3. Payment Information

Online payments are processed through the payment service provider. MilesNation does not intend to collect or store participants' complete card numbers, UPI PINs or banking passwords.

4. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with payment providers, delivery partners and service providers when necessary to operate the event, process payments or deliver medals.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or a lawful request from an authority.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect participant information against unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Requests

Participants can contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable legal and record-retention requirements.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact milesnationofficial@gmail.com.

06 / YOUR FINISHER REWARD

Medal Dispatch & Delivery Policy

MilesNation aims to deliver finisher medals to eligible participants after the completion of the event.

Dispatch: Within 1–2 working days after the event concludes.

Delivery: Within 8–10 days after dispatch, subject to courier service and location.

1. Dispatch Timeline

Eligible finisher medals will be handed over to the delivery partner within 1–2 working days after the event concludes.

2. Delivery Timeline

Delivery is expected within 8–10 days after dispatch. Actual delivery may vary depending on the participant's location, courier operations and unforeseen circumstances.

3. Tracking Information

Where tracking is available, shipment details will be shared with participants through their registered contact information.

4. Incorrect Address

Participants are responsible for providing complete and accurate delivery information during registration. Delays or failed deliveries caused by incorrect or incomplete addresses may require additional assistance.

5. Delivery Delays

If a medal is delayed beyond the expected timeline, participants can contact MilesNation support with their registration details.

6. Undelivered or Damaged Packages

Participants should report damaged, missing or undelivered packages to MilesNation as soon as possible so the matter can be checked with the delivery partner.

Policy information should be read together with the specific instructions published for each MilesNation event.
